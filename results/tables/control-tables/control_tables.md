**How to read.** A cell with one run: mean ± 95% task-bootstrap half-width (the paper's interval). A cell with several runs: their equal-weight mean ± the pooled interval, then each run's mean in parentheses, in the order given under the table ("Runs behind each cell", which also gives every run's size). ‡ ≥3% of the run's trials unscored · † a number from elsewhere (no interval) · ⏳ not available yet · — not run. Rows or columns marked as derived are computed from the cells above them. Paths and checksums: manifest.csv.

The rehearsal 2×2 and the distillation ladders over training runs are in the seed tables (`exp seed-tables`).
